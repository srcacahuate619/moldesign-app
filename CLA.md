> **Resumen en castellano (no sustituye al texto legal).** Al contribuir sigues siendo
> el dueño de tu código: no nos lo cedes. Lo que nos das es un permiso amplio para
> usarlo, incluido el derecho a distribuirlo **bajo otra licencia en el futuro**. Eso es
> lo que permite que el proyecto pueda cambiar de licencia o vender una licencia
> comercial sin tener que localizar a cada persona que alguna vez mandó un parche. Sin
> esto, la primera contribución externa congela la licencia del proyecto para siempre.

## Why this exists

MolDesign distributes source code and models under different terms, and expects to offer
commercial licences to organisations whose use falls outside the public terms. Both
things require that the project be able to license **all** of its code, including
contributed code.

An "inbound = outbound" notice alone does not achieve this: it licenses your contribution
to the world under the project licence, but it does not grant the maintainer the right to
offer your contribution under different terms. This agreement adds that right, and
nothing else.

**You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment.

## How to sign

Add a line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in your first pull request:

```text
- Full Name <email> — CLA v1.0 accepted YYYY-MM-DD (GitHub: @handle)
```

and include this trailer in the commit:

```text
MolDesign-CLA: accepted v1.0
```

Signing once covers all your future Contributions to this project, unless you notify the
maintainer otherwise in writing.

Contributions merged **before** the date this file was added to the repository are
licensed only under the project's public licence at the time of the merge, and are not
covered by section 3. See `docs/LICENSING.md` for how those are tracked.
