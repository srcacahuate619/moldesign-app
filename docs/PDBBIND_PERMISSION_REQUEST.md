# Solicitud de autorización para derivados de PDBbind v2020

Usar este borrador sólo después de identificar el titular y el contacto vigente. Conservar la respuesta completa como evidencia privada.

## English request

**Subject:** Permission request for distribution of trained model weights and aggregate validation artifacts derived from PDBbind v2020

Dear PDBbind team,

My name is Johan Amezcua and I am the author of MolDesign, a Windows desktop application for local, auditable structural-computational research. I used PDBbind v2020 material obtained through Zenodo record 7014096 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7014096), described as reprocessed with Schrödinger Protein Preparation Wizard, to train and evaluate several lightweight scoring models.

The Zenodo record currently shows no license in its Rights field. Before publishing MolDesign, I am requesting explicit written clarification and permission. I do not intend to redistribute the raw PDBbind dataset or its structure archive.

Please confirm whether I may:

1. distribute trained model weight files that do not expose individual database records;
2. publish aggregate performance reports, manifests, hashes and reproducibility metadata;
3. publish limited benchmark tables or identifiers, and under what restrictions;
4. distribute those artifacts through GitHub, Hugging Face and the Microsoft Store;
5. license MolDesign for free non-commercial use under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 while separately offering commercial licenses; and
6. allow licensees to run inference commercially under a separate agreement without receiving the PDBbind source data.

Please also clarify whether your authorization can cover the specific Schrödinger-prepared copy in Zenodo record 7014096, or whether permission from its depositor and/or another rights holder is additionally required. If attribution, citation, notice, access control, revenue-sharing, or other conditions apply, please state them explicitly.

I can provide an exact inventory of affected models, training splits and intended release artifacts for review. No public release of the affected artifacts will occur until their status is resolved.

Sincerely,

Johan Amezcua
moldesign@amezcua-dev.com
https://github.com/srcacahuate619/moldesign-app

## Resumen interno en español

La autorización debe nombrar de forma inequívoca: la versión y copia del dataset, pesos entrenados, resultados agregados, tablas limitadas, uso no comercial, licenciamiento comercial separado y los tres canales de distribución. Una respuesta que sólo diga “puede usar PDBbind para investigación” no autoriza necesariamente redistribuir pesos ni comercializar inferencia.
